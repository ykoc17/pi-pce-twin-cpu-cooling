        -| node[pos=0.25,below,font=\footnotesize]{repeat after an appropriate decision interval $\Delta t$} (measure.south);
    \end{tikzpicture}
  \end{center}
  \medskip
  \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
    \column{0.48\textwidth}
      \dataheading{Keep the model's time scale clear}
      \footnotesize
      Fast evaluation does not mean instant thermal settling.
      Choose decision timing and change limits using hardware dynamics;
      five-minute archive spacing is not an online control-rate specification.
    \column{0.49\textwidth}
      \dataheading{Separate faster safety controller}
      \footnotesize
      Monitor measured temperatures. Near the limit, override recommendations,
      reduce CPU power and use a validated fallback.
      Prediction margins alone cannot prevent transient overshoots.
  \end{columns}
  \datasource{Proposed live architecture; not an implemented or experimentally demonstrated controller.
    Temperature sensors remain active. Thermal response, command mapping and control rates require validation.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[more-space]{The whole digital twin workflow}
  \dataheading{Slower learning loop: collect, validate, then update}
  \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
    \column{0.48\textwidth}
      \dataheading{From new readings to a candidate model}
      \footnotesize
      \textbf{1. Collect} measurements continuously; preserve timestamps and provenance.\par
      \smallskip
      \textbf{2. Select} stable input periods, discard settling and require complete,
      stable CPU temperatures. Average each accepted interval into one observation.\par
      \smallskip
      \textbf{3. Fit} candidate degree-1/2 PCEs when sufficient new observations exist;
      compare the physics-guided variant and optional GP.
    \column{0.49\textwidth}
      \dataheading{Refine the data, not just the polynomial}
      \footnotesize
      \textbf{4. Validate} on entire cases; inspect global errors and local behavior
      near the thermal limit and proposed optimum.\par
      \smallskip
      \textbf{5. Refine} missing combinations if new experiments are possible.
      An optimized Latin hypercube (LHS) is a future design option within feasible,
      safe conditions; existing HEATWISE measurements were not generated by our LHS.\par
      \smallskip
      Confirm the final decision with an independent full-model or experimental check.
  \end{columns}
  \medskip
  \begin{beamercolorbox}[sep=1.5mm,wd=\textwidth]{data panel}
    \footnotesize
    \textbf{Deployment gate}\quad Replace the current model only after improved
    held-out accuracy, physical plausibility and safety checks.
    The fast loop continues using the existing validated model during retraining;
    do not refit after every sensor reading.
  \end{beamercolorbox}
  \datasource{Proposed maintenance and experimental-design workflow from the notes.
    Current implemented selection: configs/preprocessing\_config.py and results/preprocessing/report.md.
    Updates, new experiments and model replacement remain future work.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[more-space]{The whole digital twin workflow}
  \dataheading{Infrastructure and deployment concept}
  {\footnotesize
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.05}
  \begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.23\textwidth}p{0.73\textwidth}@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Layer} & \textbf{Proposed implementation and responsibility}\\
    \midrule
    Acquisition & Server sensors/monitoring interfaces: inlet temperature, CPU temperatures/power and fan settings.\\
    Communication & Data-center network; MQTT or OPC UA for telemetry/commands where hardware interfaces support them.\\
    Storage & Time-series database, e.g.\ InfluxDB: measurements, commands and model versions for monitoring/retraining.\\
    Offline modeling & Python, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn and ETH UQLab.py: fitting, validation, UQ, sensitivity and optimization.\\
    Online execution & Validated PCE/optimizer on a local server or edge device; no GPU/HPC needed for this small surrogate.\\
    Actuation + safety & Supported hardware-management commands; an independent faster safety controller can override settings.\\
    Maintenance & Retrain from new steady intervals; validate/version before replacement and retain a fallback model.\\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  }

  \begin{beamercolorbox}[sep=1.5mm,wd=\textwidth]{data warning}

    the deployment stack are conceptual; they are not connected in this project.
  \end{beamercolorbox}
  \datasource{Proposed architecture from the notes; protocols/products are examples, not installed services.
