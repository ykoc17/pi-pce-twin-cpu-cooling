\section{Uncertainty Quantification}

\begin{frame}[more-space]{Uncertainty Quantification}
  \dataheading{How many model evaluations do we need after fitting?}
  \begin{beamercolorbox}[sep=2mm,wd=\textwidth]{data panel}
    \footnotesize
    With our orthonormal PCE and the independent uniform reference (Model A),
    the output mean and variance follow directly from the coefficients:
    \[
      \mathbb{E}[\widehat{T}_{\max}^{\mathrm{ss}}]=c_{\mathbf{0}},
      \qquad
      \mathrm{Var}[\widehat{T}_{\max}^{\mathrm{ss}}]
      =\sum_{\boldsymbol{\alpha}\neq\mathbf{0}}c_{\boldsymbol{\alpha}}^2.
    \]
  \end{beamercolorbox}
  \medskip
  {\footnotesize
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
  \begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.43\textwidth}p{0.53\textwidth}@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Requested result} & \textbf{Additional PCE evaluations}\\
    \midrule
    Mean and variance & \textbf{0}: read and combine the fitted coefficients.\\
    Distribution, quantiles or probability of exceeding a temperature limit &
      Sample the surrogate: e.g.\ start with \textbf{10,000 predictions},
      then increase until the desired statistics converge.\\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  }
  \medskip
  \begin{beamercolorbox}[sep=1.5mm,wd=\textwidth]{data warning}
    \footnotesize
    This describes variation under the \textbf{assumed input distributions},
    not automatically prediction error. The sample count is illustrative;
    rare exceedances may need many more samples. More surrogate runs add no experimental evidence.
  \end{beamercolorbox}
  \datasource{Theory: \href{https://uploads-ssl.webflow.com/5f9818a73007a0ae56f4ee49/638f5915433726a0f2f46821_UserManual_PolynomialChaos.pdf}{ETH UQ[py]Lab PCE manual}, Sec. 1.6.
    Planned calculations after predictive fitting and validation; no output-UQ results yet.}
\end{frame}

\section{Sobol Sensitivity Analysis}

\begin{frame}[more-space]{Sobol Sensitivity Analysis}
  \dataheading{Sensitivity directly from the fitted PCE coefficients}
  \begin{beamercolorbox}[sep=2mm,wd=\textwidth]{data panel}
    \footnotesize
    For independent Model A inputs, each polynomial term belongs to an input
    or an interaction. Group its squared coefficient to obtain Sobol indices.
    \[
      V=\sum_{\boldsymbol{\alpha}\neq\mathbf{0}}c_{\boldsymbol{\alpha}}^2,
      \qquad
      S_i=\frac{\sum_{\alpha_i>0,\,\alpha_{j\neq i}=0}c_{\boldsymbol{\alpha}}^2}{V},
      \qquad
      S_i^{\mathrm{T}}=\frac{\sum_{\alpha_i>0}c_{\boldsymbol{\alpha}}^2}{V}.
    \]
    These variance-based indices require $V>0$.
  \end{beamercolorbox}
  \medskip
  \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
    \column{0.48\textwidth}
      \dataheading{What do the indices mean?}
      \footnotesize
      \textbf{First-order $S_i$:} the fraction of output variance attributed to
      input $i$ acting alone.\par
      \smallskip
      \textbf{Total $S_i^{\mathrm{T}}$:} the fraction involving input $i$,
      including its interactions with the other inputs.

    \column{0.49\textwidth}
      \dataheading{How many runs?}
      \footnotesize
      \textbf{0 additional PCE evaluations after fitting.}\par
      \smallskip
      Only coefficient calculations are needed: no Monte Carlo evaluation of
      the surrogate and no new physical experiments.\par
      \smallskip
      Input elimination is optional; keep all three unless evidence supports removal.
  \end{columns}
  \medskip
  \begin{beamercolorbox}[sep=1.5mm,wd=\textwidth]{data warning}
    \footnotesize
    These indices describe our \textbf{independent reference}, not the experimental
    dependence between inlet temperature and fan power. Their accuracy also depends
    on the fitted surrogate. With a dependence transformation, indices describe latent
    variables, not automatically the original physical controls. They do not establish causal effects.
  \end{beamercolorbox}
  \datasource{Theory: \href{https://sudret.ibk.ethz.ch/publications/preprints-archive/2016-007.html}{Le Gratiet, Marelli and Sudret (2016), ETH Z\"urich}.
    Model A: results/inputs/reference\_model.json. Sensitivity calculations remain planned.}
\end{frame}

      \begin{beamercolorbox}[sep=2mm,wd=\linewidth]{data panel}

        \smallskip

        documented thresholds and diagnostics.\par
        \smallskip
        Three assumed uniform input distributions with ETH UQLab.py;
        explicit separation of independent reference A and optional scenario B.
      \end{beamercolorbox}
    \column{0.49\textwidth}
      \begin{beamercolorbox}[sep=2mm,wd=\linewidth]{data warning}
        \dataheading{Next implementation and evidence}
        \footnotesize
        Fit/validate degree-1/2 PCE; compare physics-guided PCE and optionally GP.\par
        \smallskip
        Define operating disturbance uncertainty; compute UQ/Sobol;
        demonstrate optimization within supported conditions.\par
        \smallskip
        Verify fan units, command mapping and missing combinations.
        Live control requires transient tests and independent safety protection.
      \end{beamercolorbox}
  \end{columns}
  \medskip
  \begin{beamercolorbox}[sep=2mm,wd=\textwidth]{data panel}
    \footnotesize

    surrogate and repeated UQ/sensitivity/optimization tasks with two-way data
    exchange. The offline part is the implementation scope; infrastructure and live control remain conceptual.
  \end{beamercolorbox}
